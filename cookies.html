Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

This website doesn’t use cookies, and it doesn’t use similar technologies such as tracking pixels, local storage for tracking, analytics or advertising tools. That’s why we don’t show a cookie banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

What does load in your browser

  • The pages, pictures, the short video, the fonts and the code that runs the animated story. All of them come from this website itself; nothing is loaded from other companies’ servers.
  • No social media buttons, embedded videos, maps or other third-party content that could track you.
  • The optional sound is generated in your browser and isn’t remembered between visits.

Our hosting provider

The website is hosted by Vercel. To protect the website against attacks, the hosting platform may in rare cases set a strictly necessary security cookie (for example while checking that a visitor isn’t a bot). Strictly necessary cookies don’t require consent under EU and Romanian law (Law no. 506/2004). They are not used for tracking or advertising.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting them.

Questions

Write to lucacprediger@gmail.com. More about how we handle data is in our privacy policy.
