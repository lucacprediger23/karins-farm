Mafia… for kids. A card game born on a rainy day at summer camp.

01 · Summer camp

Every summer, the forest of Băneasa fills with kids. This is Karin’s Summer Camp, in Bucharest.

Then it started to pour. Every game we had planned was washed out.

03 · Shelter

We ran for cover and sat down in a circle.

04 · The question

The kids wanted to play Mafia. But Mafia is a game for grown-ups. How could it work for them?

05 · The idea

Build the game from the things the kids already know and love.

06 · The yard

The animals from our yard. The kids feed them every day.

07 · The fox

One night, the fox sneaked into our yard. Can you find him?

08 · The survivor

He came back five times. After the last raid, only the Duck was left. Hence the eye patch.

09 · Blu

Blu has been at every camp for five years. The kids say she was the Hobbit’s dog, 500 years ago.

10 · The Hobbit

He once protected our forest, and his house is still in the yard. Nobody has ever seen his face. He only writes the children letters.

11 · Karin

Grandma Karin tells the story. The academy carries her name. In the game, she is the Narrator.

12 · After the rain

Nobody wanted to stop. About 100 kids have played it since.

The game

Who’s causing trouble on the farm?

A secret-role card game for kids and families. At night, the Wild Animals cause trouble. In the morning, the farm finds out who.

6–22
Minutes

Ages
6+

  • 1 Karin’s Secret Cookbook

How to play

Night. Morning. Vote.

  1. Get a cardKeep it secret. Karin, the Narrator, runs the game.
  2. Night fallsEyes closed. The Wild Animals choose who to cause trouble for.
  3. Morning comesKarin tells what happened. Everyone talks and suspects.
  4. VoteThe player with the most votes leaves the game. Repeat until the Wild Animals are found.

Questions

Good to know

What is Karin’s Farm?

A secret-role card game for kids and families, in the spirit of Mafia. Some players are secretly Wild Animals causing trouble at night; everyone else talks, suspects and votes to find them in the morning. It was born at Karin’s Summer Camp, in the forest of Băneasa, Bucharest.

Who is it for?

Children aged 6 and up, playing with friends or family. A game takes about 30 minutes, for 6 to 22 players.

Do I need to know Mafia?

No. The illustrated rulebook explains everything, and one player, Karin the Narrator, guides the game.
