Why it works

Made for a room full of kids

  • The whole group plays

    From 6 to 22 players: a birthday party, a school class, all the cousins at once.

  • Nobody needs to know Mafia

    One player is Karin, the Narrator. The illustrated rulebook guides her through every night and morning.

  • Who is the Fox?

    Secret roles keep everyone guessing until the last vote. And at about 30 minutes, there’s always time for one more.

  • No screens, just cards

    The game is talking, suspecting and voting, out loud, around a table or in a circle on the floor.

How to play

Night. Morning. Vote.

  1. Get a cardKeep it secret. Karin, the Narrator, runs the game.
  2. Night fallsEyes closed. The Wild Animals choose who to cause trouble for.
  3. Morning comesKarin tells what happened. Everyone talks and suspects.
  4. VoteThe player with the most votes leaves the game. Repeat until the Wild Animals are found.

Questions

Good to know

What is Karin’s Farm?

A secret-role card game for kids and families, in the spirit of Mafia. Some players are secretly Wild Animals causing trouble at night; everyone else talks, suspects and votes to find them in the morning. It was born at Karin’s Summer Camp, in the forest of Băneasa, Bucharest.

Who is it for?

Children aged 6 and up, playing with friends or family. A game takes about 30 minutes, for 6 to 22 players.

Do I need to know Mafia?

No. The illustrated rulebook explains everything, and one player, Karin the Narrator, guides the game.
