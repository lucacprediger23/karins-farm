Be first to know when Karin’s Farm is out

Karin’s Farm is a secret-role card game for kids aged 6+ and their families, born at Karin’s Summer Camp in Bucharest. It isn’t on sale yet. Leave your email and you’ll hear the moment it is.

What you get

  • One email when Karin’s Farm is available, with the price and the launch date.
  • Nothing else: no newsletter, no spam, no sharing with anyone.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time with one email.

What happens next

  1. You tick the box and press Join the waitlist.
  2. You see “You’re on the list” right away.
  3. When the game is out, you get one email from us.

What we keep

  • Only your email, and the date and wording of your consent.
  • Stored privately in the EU (Frankfurt), on Vercel.
  • No cookies, no tracking. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions? Write to lucacprediger@gmail.com. The site is run by Luca Prediger, Bucharest, Romania.

Born at Karin’s Kids Academy and Karin’s Summer Camp, in the forest of Băneasa, Bucharest.
