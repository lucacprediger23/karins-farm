Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Karin’s Farm website collects, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Luca Prediger, Bucharest, Romania (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at lucacprediger@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address;
  • a record of your consent: the wording you agreed to, its version and the date and time you joined.

We don’t ask for your name, phone number, address or anything else, and we don’t store your IP address with your signup.

When you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data needed to deliver the pages and protect the site (for example your IP address, browser type and the time of the request) in short-lived server logs. We don’t use this data to identify or profile you.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don’t use cookies. See our cookie policy.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • Waitlist email: only to tell you when Karin’s Farm is available, and to answer you if you write to us. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We don’t send other marketing unless you separately agree to it.
  • Technical server data: to run the website securely and prevent abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We never sell your data, and we don’t make automated decisions about you or build profiles.

Age

The waitlist is for people aged 16 or older. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian to sign up. If we learn that someone under 16 has joined, we delete their email.

Who processes the data for us

The website and the waitlist are hosted by Vercel Inc., which acts as our processor under its data processing agreement. Waitlist records are stored in Vercel’s data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Vercel is a US company; where data may be accessed from outside the EU, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

When we email the waitlist, we may use an email service provider as a processor. If so, we will name it in this policy before using it.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and at most until 12 months after Karin’s Farm goes on sale. If the game hasn’t gone on sale within 24 months of your signup, we delete your email then. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider only for a short period.

Your rights

You can at any time:

  • withdraw your consent and unsubscribe, by writing to lucacprediger@gmail.com or using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. This doesn’t affect anything we did before you withdrew;
  • ask for access to your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted;
  • ask us to restrict its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • ask for a copy in a portable format (data portability).

We answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Romanian data protection authority: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral Gheorghe Magheru 28–30, Sector 1, București, www.dataprotection.ro, anspdcp@dataprotection.ro.

Photos on this website

Some photos on this website were taken at Karin’s Summer Camp and are published with permission. If you appear in a photo, or are the parent of a child who does, and would like it removed, write to lucacprediger@gmail.com and we will remove it promptly.

Security

Waitlist records are stored in private storage that isn’t publicly accessible, and the website is only served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and, where the change matters to you, tell you before it applies.
